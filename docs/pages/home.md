# Home

The everyday view for finding products and choosing shelves to audit. Available to Auditors
and Administrators. Designed for a tablet.

## Current page

**Status: built.**

`/` shows "No floor plans are ready yet" with a link to Floor Plans while no floor plan is
Done, and a placeholder otherwise.

## Floor view

**Status: planned.**

- A picker chooses a building and floor. Buildings appear once they have a room.
- The floor is drawn with its shelves. Tapping a shelf opens its [shelf view](shelf-view.md).
- Shelves can be listed and sorted by last adjusted or last audited, from
  [history](../history.md).

## Product search

**Status: planned.**

- Searching by product title or SKU highlights every shelf the product is on.
- A product on several shelves highlights all of them.

## No location

**Status: planned.**

A list of products the store lists on no shelf. It opens full screen like a shelf view and
offers the same "Move to shelf" action, so lost products can be given a home.
